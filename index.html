LEARN THE FEELING

Throw the shifter.
Feel the result.

On Xbox, slide the right stick left or right across Neutral, then up or down into a gear. Press L3 to switch between direct LT control and pressure build. On keyboard, hold Ctrl to build clutch pressure, then move the shifter with your mouse. The cursor locks inside the game and hides while shifting; if the browser needs a click first, use the Lock Mouse prompt. Move left, then up for 1st; down to Neutral, right to center, then up for 3rd. Crossing center Neutral catches briefly. A quick sweep stops there; keep moving sideways after the catch to reach the far lane. Any deliberate clutch press allows a shift. Shifting before the bite marker causes more wear, shifting past it causes less, and a fully depressed pedal gives a clean shift. Ease off the clutch before adding hard throttle: hard gas through the release window near the bite point scuffs it. A gripping clutch below bite does not wear; actual slipping still does. A worn clutch bites higher and grips less.
